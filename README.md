# Hi, I'm Yoohyun 👋

I’m interested in the connection between people, data, and the interfaces we use every day.

I build web experiences, work with data, and use AI coding tools to explore ideas and bring them to life. I enjoy moving between the structure behind a product and the details people actually see and interact with.

## What I work with

**Web development**  
React · Next.js · TypeScript · JavaScript · HTML · CSS

I build responsive interfaces and enjoy working on interaction, typography, layout, and motion. I’m especially interested in how small decisions—clear navigation, useful feedback, or a well-timed transition—can make an experience easier to understand.

**Data & SQL**  
I’m confident working with SQL and have three months of experience working in a data role. I enjoy querying and organizing data, understanding how it fits together, and finding the information needed to answer a question.

Working with data also shapes how I think about interfaces: what information people need, how it should be organized, and what will help them decide what to do next.

**AI-assisted development**  
I use AI coding tools as part of an iterative process: shaping an idea, building a working version, checking the result, and refining the details. I’m interested in using these tools to explore unfamiliar techniques and connect different parts of a project.

## What I care about

My goal is to build **interfaces that feel natural and approachable**.

To me, that means giving people a clear place to start, making actions understandable, and helping them feel comfortable exploring. Visual character matters to me, alongside accessibility, readable content, and predictable behavior.

I want to bring thoughtful design and well-structured data together to make digital experiences that feel friendly and useful.

## What I’m exploring next

- Connecting web interfaces with APIs and shared data.
- Turning data into clear, useful information within a product.
- Building interactions that work comfortably across devices and input methods.
- Collaborating with other builders and learning from how they approach problems.

Some of my most involved projects are private, but they continue to shape how I think about design, development, and the people using what I build.
